# Oil-Immersed Distribution Transformer

Tianyu S(B)20 / S(B)22 oil-immersed distribution transformers are designed for utility, industrial and commercial distribution systems. The product range covers voltage levels up to 22 kV and design capacities up to 4,000 kVA.

Sealed oil-immersed construction, ONAN cooling, low-loss magnetic-core design and low-noise configurations support continuous outdoor operation. Rated voltage, tap range, impedance, terminal arrangement, monitoring, accessories and site conditions can be engineered to the project specification.
