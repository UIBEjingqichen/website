# Cast Resin Dry-Type Transformer

Tianyu SCB18 cast-resin dry-type transformers are designed for indoor distribution in commercial buildings, hospitals, charging infrastructure, industrial facilities and public infrastructure. Cast-resin insulation provides an oil-free transformer platform with configurable cooling, enclosure and temperature-monitoring arrangements.

## Tested reference configurations

| Model | Rated power | Rated voltage |
|---|---:|---:|
| SCB18-1000/10-NX1 | 1000 kVA | 10 kV |
| SCB18-2500/10 | 2500 kVA | 10 kV |

## Configuration options

- AN or AF cooling
- Protective enclosure by project
- Temperature monitoring and fan control
- Vector group and cable interfaces by project specification
- Site-specific ventilation and installation requirements
