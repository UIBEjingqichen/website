# European-Type Prefabricated Substation

Tianyu European-Type Prefabricated Substations integrate high-voltage primary equipment, a dry-type transformer and low-voltage distribution equipment in a factory-assembled outdoor enclosure. Primary-voltage configurations include 6 kV, 12 kV and 35 kV, with secondary-voltage schemes selected for the project.

The series extends to 12.5 MVA. Protection, metering, monitoring, auxiliary power, cable entry, remote-operation functions and enclosure arrangement are engineered to the project single-line diagram and site requirements.
