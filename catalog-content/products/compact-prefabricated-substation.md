# Compact Prefabricated Substation

Tianyu Compact Prefabricated Substations integrate high-voltage equipment, an oil-immersed transformer and low-voltage distribution in a factory-assembled outdoor enclosure. The design is suited to renewable-energy, utility and industrial projects requiring high capacity, compact footprint and reduced field assembly.

The series extends to 15 MVA. Protection, monitoring, cable interfaces, corrosion protection, ventilation, sealing, structural loading and auxiliary systems are selected according to the electrical scheme and installation environment.
