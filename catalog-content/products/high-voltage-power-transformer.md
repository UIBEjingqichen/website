# High-Voltage Power Transformer

Tianyu supplies three-phase oil-immersed power transformers for main substations, grid interconnection and major industrial power systems. Documented reference ratings cover 110 kV, 132 kV and 220 kV equipment, with tested capacities from 50 MVA to 240 MVA.

Each transformer is engineered around the project electrical system and site conditions. Voltage ratio, vector group, impedance, tap-changing range, insulation level, guaranteed losses, cooling arrangement, monitoring, accessories and transport limits are confirmed in the technical specification and approved drawings before production.

## Engineering scope

- Insulation coordination
- On-load tap-changing arrangements where specified
- ONAN / ONAF cooling according to design
- Loss and temperature-rise guarantees
- Monitoring and protection interfaces
- Mechanical and transport engineering
