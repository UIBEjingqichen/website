# Product Media Classification v43

This file records the v43 reconciliation of `source-media/products`, especially the newly uploaded `source-media/products/products` folder. Existing semantic folders remain the canonical source library. Duplicate uploads are resolved by Git blob identity, nameplates are kept as technical evidence rather than hero imagery, videos are excluded from image carousels, and the prohibited PV photograph is removed and blacklisted.

## Canonical product-media families

- `power-transformers/`: oil-immersed power transformer product views and installed views.
- `distribution-transformers/`: oil-immersed distribution transformer product and factory views.
- `dry-type-transformers/`: cast-resin, amorphous-alloy and dry-type product / factory views.
- `combined-transformers/`: American-type combined transformer exterior and interior views.
- `prefabricated-substations/`: YB / YBH / ZGS / integrated PV-ESS product, render and compartment views.
- `special-transformers/`: rectifier and mobile-substation reference product imagery.
- `switchgear/`: HV / MV / LV switchgear and RMU media; kept for substation engineering context rather than transformer hero carousels.

## Product-page composition rules

1. Hero carousels use product media only. Application landscapes are not used as product identity images.
2. The prohibited floating-solar / combined-transformer photograph is removed from source aliases and generated assets.
3. Exact-voltage images are used on the matching voltage page when available. 132 kV images remain classified but are not mislabelled as 66, 110 or 220 kV.
4. Nameplates remain technical evidence and are not mixed into product hero carousels.
5. Existing semantic folders are preferred over duplicate uploads. Git blob identity is used to deduplicate equivalent files.
6. Product pages without an exact photographed model use a family/reference product view rather than inventing model-specific evidence.
7. PV / Converter / ESS uses the catalog energy-storage converter-booster product image plus integrated-substation product renders, replacing the prohibited site photograph and generic renewable landscapes.
