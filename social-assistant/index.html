Tianyu Social Assistant

Prepare industrial video posts locally.
Review every draft before uploading.

Developed and operated by Tianyu Electric, Tianyu Social Assistant is a Windows application for its own authorized operator. It helps turn a local industrial video library into reviewed social media drafts.

The application runs on the operator's computer. This website provides application information and privacy disclosures; it does not offer public account registration or an online software service.

From local video to reviewed draft

  1. Analyze selected material. The application indexes the local video library and uses video metadata, existing descriptions and a small set of sampled frames to help plan content.
  2. Prepare platform-specific copy. AI assistance creates English drafts for Facebook, LinkedIn and YouTube. Videos, drafts, revisions and review decisions remain in the local review workspace.
  3. Review and edit. The operator previews the full video, checks the proposed content, edits the copy and approves each platform separately.
  4. Confirm an upload. After approval, the operator separately confirms the target YouTube channel and visibility before a video can be uploaded through the YouTube API.

Google authorization for YouTube

The application uses Google OAuth and YouTube API Services only after the operator grants access to their Google account. Google handles sign-in; the application does not ask for or store a Google password.

  • youtube.upload allows an explicitly confirmed video upload with its title, description, tags and selected visibility.
  • youtube.readonly identifies the connected channel and reads the status needed to verify uploads and processing.

By using the application's YouTube features, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Details of data access, external AI processing, local storage and authorization removal are explained in the Privacy Policy.

Approval and uploading are separate

Saving a draft, approving copy or running the weekly planning task does not upload or publish content. A separate confirmation is required for the actual YouTube upload, including private uploads.

Human review remains essential. Sampled frames cannot establish everything in a video or its audio. The operator checks technical claims, third-party brands, personal information and media rights before confirming an upload.

Google authorization can be revoked at any time through Google Account connections. Removing local data does not remove videos already stored on YouTube.
